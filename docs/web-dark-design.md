# FitSync dark web design

The web uses Inter, forest-black surfaces, chalk-white type, and the existing lime brand color. Layout and interaction styling use Tailwind utilities; palette tokens live in `web/src/app/globals.css`.

## Original hero artwork

- Final asset: `web/public/art/athlete-motion.webp`
- Generation mode: built-in ImageGen.
- Usage: homepage hero; responsive Next Image with an accessible description.
- The generated PNG was encoded as WebP for delivery.

### Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original premium fitness website hero artwork, portrait 4:5 composition. Primary request: an exceptional editorial illustration of one athletic runner launching forward in a powerful sprint, the whole body fully visible, viewed three-quarter side, facing to the left, long confident limbs, believable athletic anatomy and dynamic bent knees, crisp black shorts and a pale electric lime (#c5f460) running singlet. Style: hand-drawn sports campaign illustration with fine white chalk and graphite contour lines, exquisite dense cross-hatching, gestural construction lines, selective solid dark silhouettes and carefully restrained lime ink. Sophisticated and art-directed, like a professional illustrator's movement study translated into a premium fashion/sports print. Backdrop must be flat very dark forest black (#0b0e0b), with just a few oversize pale lime elliptical trajectory strokes behind the athlete, quiet hand-drawn motion streaks. Main figure should dominate, filling 85 percent of image height. No text, lettering, numerals, logos, UI, grids, cards, border, watermark, or photorealistic photography. No neon glow, no gradient background, no lens effects, no generic AI sci-fi aesthetic. The image should feel tactile, human, confident and beautifully composed, with lots of actual visible precise white drawing detail rather than dark indistinct shapes.

## Code-native illustrations and interactions

- SVG strength, mobility, and bodyweight studies in the training selector.
- SVG mobile companion illustration with clearly marked example content.
- Interactive 3D dumbbell studio with drag, keyboard, weight, and motion controls.
- Interactive weekly rhythm preview with training frequency and day selection.

Motion honors reduced-motion settings. The weekly schedule and phone illustration are examples, not saved user progress.
