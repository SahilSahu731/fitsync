# FitSync authentication redesign

Login and signup share a responsive dark layout with distinct headings, a focused Google sign-in action, readable status messages, and the existing account/workspace path. Styling uses Tailwind and the existing Inter and lime theme. Page metadata identifies each route independently.

## Original artwork

- Final asset: `web/public/art/auth-ready.webp`
- Generation mode: built-in ImageGen.
- The generated PNG was encoded as WebP for delivery.
- Used on both `/login` and `/register`, with a shorter image panel on mobile.
- The image replaces the previous `/auth-image.png` reference.

### Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create an original high-end sports editorial photograph for the full-height portrait image panel on a fitness website login and signup page. Portrait 4:5 composition. A fit adult Black woman athlete sits on a low dark concrete bench in a quiet minimalist training studio, leaning forward to tie the laces of her running shoe before a session. Thoughtful, grounded, quietly determined moment, believable anatomy, natural hands and fingers, athletic rather than fashion posing. Three-quarter side view, full figure and both shoes visible, figure occupies the central 60 percent of the composition. Outfit is charcoal running shorts and a simple black sleeveless training top, white crew socks, shoes with restrained pale electric lime (#c5f460) accents and lime laces. Scene is almost entirely monochrome, true matte black and rich charcoal gray tones with soft silvery side window light and one shaft of daylight on the textured concrete floor. Tactile fine film grain, realistic skin and fabric detail, professional 50mm editorial sports campaign photograph, strong sculptural contrast, subdued gym environment with one out-of-focus weight and no clutter. Reserve quiet dark negative space across the top 18 percent and bottom 20 percent for website text added later in code. Subject should be large and legible, not tiny in a cavernous room. Palette must harmonize with #0b0e0b forest black and #c5f460 lime. No text, typography, branding, logos, watermark, UI, collage, neon glow, sci-fi aesthetics, fake screen, or excessive gym machinery. Make it feel human, premium, and calm: the moment before your next good session.
