# Train and workout tracking

Train keeps starting or resuming a workout prominent. Saved Programs and Routines are independent disclosure sections, closed on initial mount, with a count and an arrow. Active program guidance stays visible, personal coaching is compact, and the empty program promotion is omitted. Expanded routines can be searched. Recent workouts initially show five entries and can reveal more of the existing 100-entry history response. Weekly totals use the device's local Monday-to-Sunday calendar week and the completion date, matching Progress.

The workout screen puts its overview and notes in the scrollable content so exercises have more room. An active rest timer stays above the list. Users can start a timer manually, extend or skip it, view exercise instructions, change set types, record notes and effort, undo a logged set, and remove an individual set. The delete-set endpoint verifies ownership and that the workout is active. Small screens omit the compact number fields' steppers to leave more room for typed values.

Valid metric edits are written to the account's device journal immediately, including edits to unlogged sets. Logging still requires an explicit tap; prefilled targets do not count as performed work. Reps and duration must be positive to log a set. Bodyweight exercises allow zero or omitted added load. Set-specific rest overrides remain device preferences, as labelled in the editor.

Finishing waits for outstanding device writes and queues completion after edits and notes. A review shows logged performance and the number of unlogged sets excluded from results. The completion summary is available from the local journal while offline. Strength volume follows Progress: only logged working sets with external load contribute; warm-ups and bodyweight load are excluded. Upload retries preserve the original duration and completion date, and a new workout waits until pending completion has synced. A successful upload invalidates Train history, followed programs, and personal coaching. Refresh responses cannot replace an in-flight structural workout edit. Rest begins immediately, independently of notification permission, and restored timers belong to a specific workout.

Regression coverage includes journal revision conflicts, queued completion, writes racing with finishing, subscriber invalidation after upload, timer cancellation during permission requests, structural refresh races, summary totals, week boundaries, and set-removal ownership/status checks. Native device and visual QA still need to be performed; this session's preview browser was unavailable.
